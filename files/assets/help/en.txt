[[tools]] Tools

[reboot] Reboot
Restarts the device.

[cache] Cache library
Caches the library.

Besides this button, caching also happens as you use the player.

[scan] Update library
Re-reads and updates metadata and cover art for songs already in the library, in case they have changed.

[backup] Data backup
Saves the settings, likes, playlists, bookmarks and reading progress to the /better-Y/ folder on the SD card, and reads them back. Save writes a new archive, Load offers the ones already on the card.

An update through the Updater rewrites usrdata and takes all of that with it; the SD card is left alone, so a copy that lives there survives. Installing the APK over adb, or flashing the ANDROID partition alone, keeps the data anyway.
---
An archive holds the settings of the player and of better-Y, the library with its playlists and bookmarks, the reading progress and the album covers picked by hand. The themes, the music and the cover caches are not in it.

The five newest archives are kept. After Load the app restarts.

[log] Save diagnostic log
Saves a diagnostic report to the /better-Y/ folder on the SD card for a bug report.

[sf] Restart SurfaceFlinger
A diagnostic: restarts the compositor and writes a report to the /better-Y/ folder.

This option is only there in debug mode, which is switched on by five centre presses on Settings → About.

[first_artist_only] Show only the first artist
Hides everything after the first comma in the artist tag, leaving only the first artist. For example:
 
"Tree, Shelf Nunny, Lena Kuhn — People" 
becomes
"Tree — People"

Works only in the artist line of the player.

Hidden artists can be moved to the title by enabling "{row:feat_in_title}".
@img first_artist_only_off.png {Off}
@img first_artist_only_on.png {On}

[feat_in_title if first_artist_only] Move hidden artists to the title
Moves the artists hidden by "{row:first_artist_only}" into the song title, after a "feat.". For example:

"Tree, Shelf Nunny, Lena Kuhn — People"
with this setting Off becomes
"Tree — People"

with this setting On becomes
"Tree — People (feat. Shelf Nunny, Lena Kuhn)"

Works only in the artist line of the player.
@img first_artist_only_on.png {On} + {Off}
@img feat_in_title_on.png {On} + {On}

[[menu]] Menu

[alpha_scroll] Alphabetical fast scroll
Starts after a number of wheel clicks and jumps through a list by first letter.

Does not apply when the list is sorted by file name.

With "{row:meta_title}" — {Off} the rows show file names, but the jump still goes by the titles from the tags: that is the order the list is really sorted in.

Songs with no title tag are one group at the end of the list, shown as "#".

[alpha_threshold if alpha_scroll] Activation threshold (number of scrolls)
The number of wheel scrolls after which "{row:alpha_scroll}" starts.

[follow_playing] Selection follows playing song
When the screen is locked, and while the scroll wheel is at rest, the selection automatically follows the song currently playing.

The selection switches to manual mode as soon as the scroll wheel is used. Once scrolling stops, a timer starts; after it expires, the selection moves to the currently playing song, and automatic mode resumes. Opening the context menu or MultiSelect mode resets the timer and pauses it.

When you open the list containing the currently playing song, the selection immediately moves to it. Exiting the player also sets the selection to it.

[follow_idle if follow_playing] Return delay (seconds)
Determines how many seconds after the last scroll of the wheel the selection returns to the song currently playing.

[fixed_menu_pad] Fixed menu margins
Fixes margins of the main menu and settings.

Worth turning off with themes whose backgrounds are drawn for stock margins — bundled Melody Munchkin, for example.

[[metadata]] Metadata

[meta_title] Song titles from metadata
Shows song titles from the metadata instead of the file name.

[book_meta_title] Audiobook titles from metadata
Shows audiobook titles from the metadata instead of the file name.

[album_year] Release year in album names
Shows the album's release year in its name. For example:
"The Eminem Show (2002)"

[track_numbers] Track numbers from metadata inside albums
Shows track numbers from the metadata inside albums. 

It applies whatever the sort is: if sorted by file name - the numbers won't be in order.
---
If no track numbers are specified for the songs on the album, the stock numbers are used instead.

If track numbers are specified for only some of the songs, the songs without numbers will be moved to the bottom, and a "#" will appear in place of their track numbers.

[artist_split] Split artists that are divided by commas and semicolons
Splits artists that are listed in the metadata with commas or semicolons into separate artists:

"System of a Down, RZA" or
"System of a Down; RZA"
becomes two different artists
"System of a Down" and "RZA"
---
To keep an artist whose name contains a comma, use a semicolon — it takes priority:
 
"Tyler, The Creator; Frank Ocean" 
becomes two different artists
"Tyler, The Creator" and "Frank Ocean"

Semicolons are shown as commas everywhere in the interface.
---
The same can be done with commas alone by adding the artist to /better-Y/comma_artists.txt:

"Tyler, The Creator, Frank Ocean"
becomes two different artists
"Tyler, The Creator" and "Frank Ocean"

[genre_split] Split genres that are divided by commas, semicolons and slashes
Splits genres that are listed in the metadata with commas, semicolons or slashes into separate genres:

"Indie Rock, Acoustic" or 
"Indie Rock; Acoustic" or 
"Indie Rock / Acoustic" or
"Indie Rock/Acoustic"
becomes two different genres 
"Indie Rock" and "Acoustic"

[artist_scope] Show songs only by selected artist inside of Artists - Album
When an album is opened through the "{app:artists}" section, only the songs the selected artist appears on are shown; the rest are hidden.
@img artist_scope_off.png {Off}, Beat Culture artist is selected
@img artist_scope_on.png {On}, Beat Culture artist is selected

[[system]] System

[delete_folder] After deleting a song also delete the folder it was stored in
When songs or albums are deleted, the folders that held them are deleted as well, if they are left empty.
---
A folder holding nothing but external covers (cover.* and folder.*), lyrics (*.lrc) and empty folders counts as empty and goes too.

This does not apply to the "{app:folders}" section, where deleting works as usual.

[keep_awake] Disable auto screen lock while reading lyrics and books
Keeps the screen from locking itself while the lyrics screen or a book is open.

[likes] Favorites system
Adds the "{app:favorites}" playlist and the "❤" button in the player: pressing it puts the song into that playlist.
---
You can add songs to it just like you would to a regular playlist, and likes are automatically added and removed.

When you turn off this setting, the playlist and the likes you've given aren't deleted - they're simply hidden.

[kb_lang2] Second keyboard language
* Off
* Russian
Adds a second layout to the on-screen keyboard.

Hold top button to switch.
